### New

- **Introductory tour.** On the first start, a short tour walks through the screen on the live app: games and saves, characters, the tabs, how changes show, saving, and settings. The area being explained is lit inside a frame while the rest goes dark, and the explanation sits in the dark beside it.
- The step you are on is shown ("Step 3 of 7"). You can go forward and back, and skip the tour at any time. It works with the keyboard (arrows and Esc) and with a pad (A for next, B to skip).
- While the tour is open, the app looks as it is but nothing in it can be pressed. Once finished or skipped it doesn't open by itself again; Settings › General can show it again.
