### New

- **Full gamepad control**, with no separate build or setting. Pressing a button on a pad switches the app to pad mode: the hints on screen show the connected pad's buttons (Xbox, PlayStation or Steam Deck), and a selection cursor appears as in the game's menus. Using the mouse or keyboard switches back.
- Buttons: the d-pad or left stick moves, A selects, B goes back, LB/RB switch tabs, LT/RT switch between the three panels, Start saves, View opens the settings, and the right stick scrolls.
- **On-screen keyboard**: opens when A is pressed on a text or number field in pad mode, so no physical keyboard is needed. It has a Turkish Q layout, symbols, number and hex pads, and multi-line editing for JSON.
- **In-app file browser**: in pad mode, files and folders are picked without a system dialog, with shortcuts to the save folders, installs and drives.
