### New

- **Loading screen.** While the app starts, a bonfire and what is being done replace the empty black window: looking for the installed games and their versions, looking for save files, opening the last save. Each step says what it found once it is done, for example "Scholar of the First Sin · Game 1.0.1.0 · Regulation 02000100" and "1 save found".
- The window opens once its first frame is ready, so it no longer shows as an empty black rectangle for a moment.
