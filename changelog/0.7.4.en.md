### Fixed

- At the window's minimum size (1040×660) the view on the right ran off screen: some tabs were cut off and the page could slide sideways. The two lists on the left now narrow with the window, and the views tidy up in tight space: the tabs close up, item names wrap instead of being cut, the categories shrink to icons while adding an item, and the byte view shows all 16 columns.
- The bottom bar no longer overflows in a narrow window: the long notice shown while the game runs wraps onto two lines, and the pad guide fits.
- While adding an item, the reinforcement and infusion fields no longer spill out beside the scrollbar.
