### Fixed

- On Windows the pad stopped being detected for good once the app had been clicked with the mouse. The pad is now read through XInput and always works while the app's window is in front; after using the mouse, any pad button switches the app back to pad mode.
- Note: on Windows the app now supports Xbox-compatible (XInput) pads, which include Xbox controllers and pads such as 8BitDo in XInput mode. On Windows, PlayStation pads need a tool such as Steam or DS4Windows to present them as an Xbox pad. Nothing changes on Linux and the Steam Deck.
