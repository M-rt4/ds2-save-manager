### Fixed

- Clicking the title bar while a confirmation was open counted as "No" and closed it, so a restore from a backup could silently not happen. With a dialog open the window can now be moved, minimized and maximized.
- With a pad, pressing down on a backup's row skipped the row's own buttons (Restore) and went to the next backup.
- After "Fit to the installed game" the File tab kept showing the old plan; it now checks the file again.
- On Linux, when started through Steam, each pad press could arrive twice (from the physical pad and from Steam's virtual one). Pads that Steam tells games to ignore are now ignored.
