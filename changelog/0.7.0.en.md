### New

- With a gamepad you can now tell at a glance where the cursor is: the panel, dialog or on-screen keyboard holding it glows in a bonfire-ember frame, and the other panels step back. When the cursor moves to another region, the frame follows with a short pulse.
- The active panel's name sits on its bottom edge between LT and RT, drawn with your pad's own button glyphs, showing how to reach the panels on either side.
- The highlighted item's glow and its diamond are stronger, so they read from the couch on a Steam Deck or a TV.
- Nothing changes when you use the keyboard and mouse.
